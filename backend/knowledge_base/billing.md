# Payment methods

The store accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. A card is charged only when the order ships, although the bank may show a temporary authorization when the order is placed.

# Failed payments

Verify the billing address, card number, expiration date, security code, and available balance. If payment still fails, contact the card issuer or use another supported payment method. For security, support agents cannot see or enter a customer's full card number.

# Duplicate charges

One of two identical pending entries is often a temporary authorization and normally disappears within three to five business days. If both charges have posted, contact support with the order number and the last four card digits. Never send a full card number by chat or email.
