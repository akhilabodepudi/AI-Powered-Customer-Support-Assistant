# Order tracking

Customers can track an order from Account > Orders > Track shipment. A shipping confirmation email also includes the carrier tracking link. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to show movement after the label is created.

# Order changes

An order can be cancelled or its delivery address changed only while its status is Processing. Open Account > Orders, select the order, and choose Cancel order or Edit address. Once the status changes to Shipped, the order cannot be edited or cancelled. Customer support cannot redirect a shipped package; the customer may contact the carrier after the first delivery attempt.

# Missing packages

If tracking says Delivered but the package is missing, check the building mailroom, porch, household members, and neighbors. Carriers can scan a package as delivered up to 24 hours before arrival. If it is still missing after 24 hours, contact support with the order number so a delivery investigation can be opened. Investigations usually take three to five business days.
